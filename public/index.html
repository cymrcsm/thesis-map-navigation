Kiosk Quick Guide

How to Navigate the Campus

Follow these quick steps to find your destination:

1
Search or Filter

Type an office, room code, or acronym in the search bar, or filter by category above.

2
Tap Location Pins

Touch any location pin icon on the map to view room details, office hours, and descriptions.

3
Ask for Directions

Select any destination pin and tap Ask for Directions to view an active route line from your current kiosk position.

4
Reset View

Tap the 🎯 auto-center button anytime to return to the full campus overview.
