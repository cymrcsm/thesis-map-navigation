Kiosk Quick Guide

How to Navigate the Campus

Follow these quick steps to find your destination:

1
Search or Filter

Type an office, room code (e.g., RS-101), or acronym (e.g., REGISTRAR) in the search bar, or filter by category above.

2
Tap Location Pins

Touch any location pin icon on the map to view room details, office hours, and descriptions.

3
Explore & Switch Floors

Drag the map to pan around. Use the GF / 2F / 3F floor selector at the bottom right to view multi-story buildings.

4
Reset View

Tap the 🎯 auto-center button anytime to return to the full campus overview.
